# Esoterica on your iPhone

Esoterica runs as a web app that you add to your Home Screen. It opens full screen like a normal app and needs no App Store or Apple developer account.

## Install

1. Open your Esoterica link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**. Keep the name "Esoterica" and tap **Add**.
3. From now on, open Esoterica **from that icon**, not from Safari. The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari's, so anything you set up in a Safari tab won't show up in the app.

## Set up

1. Open Esoterica from the icon and enter your name and birthday.
2. Go to **Settings** (the gear at the top of the Me or Ask tab).
3. Under **Jung AI**, paste your Claude API key, tap **Save key**, then **Test connection**. You create the key at console.anthropic.com under API keys. It is stored only on your phone and is never put in backups.
4. Optional: pick a cheaper model. Claude Opus 5.5 is the default and costs roughly 2–4¢ per chat message (about $5–20 a month with regular use). Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs about half that. Settings shows a running estimate.

## Back up every week

Everything lives only on your phone, so a backup file is your safety net.

- **Settings → Back up now → Save to Files.** Today reminds you when your last backup is more than a week old.
- **Deleting the Home Screen icon deletes Esoterica's data.** iOS can also clear website data when storage runs very low.
- **To restore** (new phone, reinstalled icon): install as above, then tap **Restore from a backup** on the welcome screen and pick your file. You can also restore from Settings. Add your API key again afterwards.

## Updates

When a new version is deployed, close Esoterica fully and reopen it to load the update. Your data stays on your phone.

## Privacy

- Your profile, journal, dreams, readings and chats are stored on your phone only.
- Chat messages go straight from your phone to Anthropic using your key. The site that serves Esoterica holds only the app's code: no data and no keys.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Your key was rejected"**: paste the key again and check that your Anthropic account has credit.
- **A message failed**: check your connection, then tap **Retry** under it.
- **You see the welcome screen again**: you probably opened Esoterica in Safari instead of from the icon, or the app's data was cleared. Use the icon, or restore from your backup.

Esoterica offers reflection, not prediction. Its Jung is an AI inspired by Carl Jung's writings: not Jung himself, and not a therapist. If you are struggling, call or text 988 (US), find a helpline at findahelpline.com, or call your local emergency number.
